# WhatsApp Outreach

Keep it short. One observation, one offer, one soft ask.

## Day 1 — opener
> Hi [Name], I came across {{where you found them}} 👋
>
> I noticed {{specific observable problem — e.g. "customers have to message you
> to ask which items are available and the prices"}}.
>
> I build simple mobile catalogs where customers can browse everything and send
> their order straight to your WhatsApp — fewer back-and-forth messages, fewer
> lost orders.
>
> Want me to send a quick demo so you can see what I mean? No obligation.

## Variant — website
> Hi [Name] 👋 I searched for {{business}} online and couldn't find a website —
> just your [IG/Facebook]. A simple site would help new customers find and trust
> you when they Google you. I can show you a demo of what it'd look like — want me to?

Notes: only ONE emoji, never a wall of text. If no reply, follow-ups in
`follow-ups.md`.
