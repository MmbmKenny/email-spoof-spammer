# LinkedIn Message Outreach

Best for B2B-ish prospects: agencies, real estate, clinics, larger services.

## Connection note
> Hi [Name] — I help small businesses in [area] set up simple websites and
> ordering tools. Came across {{business}} and had a quick idea. Happy to connect.

## First message (after connect)
> Thanks for connecting, [Name]. I noticed {{observed problem}}. I built a quick
> demo of how it could work for a business like yours — want me to share it?
> No pitch, just thought it might be useful.
