# Objection Handling

Honest, practical, no manipulation. Never invent clients or results.

### "Too expensive."
> I hear you. The intro price is kept low precisely because I'm building my
> portfolio right now. We can also start with a smaller version and add to it
> once it's paying for itself. What budget were you thinking?

### "I already have a website."
> Nice — is it doing what you need? A lot of sites look fine but don't make it
> easy to order or aren't mobile-friendly. Happy to take a quick look and tell
> you honestly if it's fine as-is or worth a refresh. No charge for the look.

### "I don't need a website."
> Fair enough — plenty of businesses run fine on WhatsApp/IG. Sometimes the
> bigger win is a catalog or ordering tool rather than a full site. If customers
> ever ask "do you have a website?", that's usually the sign. I'll leave the demo
> with you either way.

### "I'll think about it."
> Totally fine. Can I send the demo link so it's there when you're ready? And is
> there anything specific holding you back — price, timing, or not sure it'd
> help? I'd rather answer honestly than chase you.

### "Can you do it cheaper?"
> I keep prices lean already. I can fit the budget by trimming scope — e.g.
> starting with [X] and adding [Y] later — rather than cutting quality. What
> number works for you and I'll tell you honestly what fits.

### "Why should I pay when AI can build it?"
> Honestly, good tools are part of how I work fast and keep prices low. What
> you're paying for is that it's actually done — designed for your business,
> set up, deployed, and working — instead of a half-finished project on your
> to-do list. I handle the whole thing so you can run your business.

### "Send me your portfolio."
> Sure — here are live demos: [DEMO LINK]. They're demonstration builds (labelled
> as demos) so you can see the quality and style. I'm early and taking on intro-
> priced projects to build real client work — happy to make yours the next one.

### "How do I know you're legitimate?"
> Completely reasonable. A few things: (1) you can see live demos of my work
> here [DEMO LINK]; (2) I take a small deposit and only the balance on delivery,
> so you're not paying everything upfront; (3) we can start with something small
> so you can judge the quality before committing more. Does that help?
