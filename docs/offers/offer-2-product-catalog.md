# Online Product Catalog

**What is it?** A mobile-friendly catalog of your products that customers can
browse, search, and order from — no more sending photos one by one.

**Who is it for?** Businesses selling physical products: boutiques, thrift
stores, electronics, cosmetics, food sellers, home goods.

**What problem does it solve?** Customers currently DM to ask "what do you have?"
and "how much?". You repeat yourself all day and still lose sales. A catalog
answers instantly and takes the order.

**What do I get?**
- Product catalog with categories and individual product pages
- Search
- WhatsApp ordering (customer's cart becomes a WhatsApp message to you)
- Mobile optimized
- Your contact information

**How long does it take?** 3–5 days once we have your products and photos.

**How much does it cost?** ₦20,000–₦50,000+ depending on number of products and
features. Confirmed before we start.

**How do I start?** Send us your product list (or your IG page) and we'll plan it.
