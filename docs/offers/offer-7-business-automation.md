# Business Automation

**What is it?** Simple internal tools and automations that remove repetitive
manual work.

**Who is it for?** Any business doing the same manual task over and over: order
tracking, lead capture, customer follow-up, document generation, reporting.

**What problem does it solve?** Manual work is slow, error-prone, and steals
hours you could spend growing. We automate the boring parts.

**What do I get?** (scoped to your needs)
- Order management / lead capture
- Customer follow-up
- Document generation
- Reporting / simple dashboards
- Internal tools

**How long does it take?** Varies by complexity.

**How much does it cost?** Quoted by complexity. We won't underquote a complex
build just to close — we'll scope it honestly.

**How do I start?** Tell us the task that eats the most of your time. We'll see
what's automatable.
