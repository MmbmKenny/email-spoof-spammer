# Business Website

**What is it?**
A clean, professional, mobile-first website for your business — the online home
customers find when they search for you.

**Who is it for?**
Any business with no website, or an outdated/slow/broken one: restaurants,
salons, gyms, clinics, tradespeople, agencies, local services.

**What problem does it solve?**
When people can't find you online — or find something that looks unprofessional —
they go to a competitor. A proper site builds trust and brings enquiries.

**What do I get?**
- Professional responsive design (looks great on phones)
- Your services / products laid out clearly
- One-tap WhatsApp contact + contact form
- Google Maps location
- Basic SEO so you can be found
- Deployed and live

**How long does it take?** Usually 3–5 days after we have your content.

**How much does it cost?** From ₦30,000. Final price depends on pages and
features — we'll confirm before starting. No surprises.

**How do I start?** Send a message. We'll ask a few quick questions, then show
you a plan and a quote.
