# Product Ad Creative

**What is it?** Ad angles, hooks, scripts, and copy to help your products sell.

**Who is it for?** Ecommerce sellers running (or wanting to run) ads.

**What problem does it solve?** Boring or unclear ads waste money. Good angles
and hooks make people stop scrolling and buy.

**What do I get?**
- Ad angles + hooks
- Short-form video scripts
- UGC concepts
- Captions + CTAs
- Landing-page copy

**How long does it take?** 1–2 days.

**How much does it cost?** ₦10,000–₦25,000+ depending on how many angles/assets.

**How do I start?** Send us the product and who buys it. We'll write the angles.
